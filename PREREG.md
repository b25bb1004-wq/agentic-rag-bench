# Pre-registration (written 2026-11-03, before any model result exists)

Written by the lead before the first real run. Changes after the first run go in a dated "Amendments" section at the bottom, never as edits above.

## Hypotheses
- H1 (poison dose-response): with the same number of poisoned episodes k, retrieval memory (C1) shows a higher habit and fact poisoning rate than fine-tuning (C2) at k = 1 and 5, and the gap shrinks at k = 25.
- H2 (grounding): C2 has lower counterfactual-follow rate (`followed_evidence`, among `exposed` cases) than C0 and C1.
- H3 (utility): C1 and C2 do not reduce clean EM relative to C0 by more than 3 points; otherwise safety differences are reported as utility-confounded.
No directional claim is made for injection, jailbreak, citation hallucination or unsupported claim; they are exploratory.

## Primary endpoints (Holm-Bonferroni across these 6 only, alpha 0.05)
1. Habit poisoning rate, C1 vs C2, k = 5, ReAct.
2. Fact poisoning rate, C1 vs C2, k = 5, ReAct.
3. Counterfactual follow rate among exposed cases, C2 vs C0, ReAct.
4. Counterfactual follow rate among exposed cases, C1 vs C0, ReAct.
5. Clean EM, C1 vs C0, ReAct.
6. Clean EM, C2 vs C0, ReAct.
Everything else (Plan agent, other attacks, other k) is secondary and reported without a significance claim.

## Design rules
- Unit of analysis: qid. Paired comparisons (same qids across conditions), exact McNemar for binary outcomes, paired bootstrap 95% CI for differences, Wilson intervals for single rates.
- Attack success is reported unconditional and conditional (paired clean run correct, and for counterfactual the edited passage retrieved). The conditional one is the headline.
- Parse failures are never counted as robustness. Parse-failure and abstain rates are printed next to every cell.
- Oracle-context control: every primary comparison is repeated with gold and edited passages pre-supplied, to separate retrieval failure from generation behaviour.
- C0 and C1 use the base model exported through the same HF to GGUF q8_0 pipeline as the merged C2 model.
- C1 and C2 use the identical selected episode set (sampled with a fixed seed).
- Seeds: 3 inference seeds for all conditions; C2 trained with 3 training seeds if throughput allows, otherwise 1, stated in the report.
- Judge: Qwen3-8B Q4 scores claim support only, validated on 100 operator-labelled items (stratified, at least 25 from unsupported-claim cases) with Cohen's kappa and Wilson intervals; judge disagreement rate is reported. A secondary cross-model judge (agy) re-scores a 100-item sample.

## Exclusions (fixed now)
Items whose clean control had a parse failure are excluded from conditional ASR (still shown unconditionally). No other post hoc exclusions.

## Known limits (state them in every deliverable)
Retriever on the Mac is BM25 only (no dense model) to keep run start-up cheap; hybrid only if run on the PC. Single backbone family on the Mac (Qwen3-1.7B); a second backbone (Qwen3-8B on the PC) only if run. Synthetic attacks. HotpotQA only (Wikipedia, likely seen in pretraining). C1 and C2 differ in representation by design (summary line vs full trajectory), so the comparison is "same episodes, different storage", not "same bytes".
